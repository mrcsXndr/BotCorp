Approval token

      
On loopback, a change that widens what a bot may do needs the token this cockpit printed when it started ([cockpit] approval token), also in the owner-only file ~/.botcorp/state/cockpit-approve-token. It stays in this tab only. Without it, botcorp approve <bot> <id> in your terminal does the same.
