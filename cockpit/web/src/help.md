## Chats and pinned bots

A **chat** is a bot you start in one tap and archive when done. A **pinned** bot stays running.

## New bot and Keep running

Keep running pins a bot. Turn it off and the bot becomes a chat.

## Telegram and pairing

Turn Telegram on in the bot's Telegram tab. Anyone who messages it waits there until you tap Pair.

## Inbox

Everything a bot needs from you lands here. **Approve** is one tap; read what it widens first.

## Accounts

An account is a Claude login. Backups take over when a limit hits, keeping the conversation.

## Updates

The version card says Update when a release is ready. Nothing applies by itself.

## A stuck bot

Open the bot's menu and choose Restart. Still stuck: Restart fresh.

## On your phone

Add the cockpit to your home screen. Chat is the default view; Terminal shows the session itself.
